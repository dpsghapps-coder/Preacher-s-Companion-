# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Preachers, worship leaders and church members of the Presbyterian Church of Ghana. Mostly used on a phone or laptop while preparing: planning sermons, choosing hymns for a service, looking up which hymns cite a Bible passage, and reading The Shepherd's Staff. Many readers are older, so text size and tap targets matter more than density.

## Product Purpose
A private, offline-capable companion that puts the PCG hymnal (850 hymns, English and Twi side by side), gospel songs and a preaching library in one fast searchable app. Success: find the right hymn or passage in seconds and read it comfortably.

## Brand Personality
Three selectable themes, one product:
- **Reverent**: quiet, dignified, like a well-printed hymn book. The words come first.
- **Warm**: friendly and approachable, a little colour and personality.
- **Crisp**: a clean modern tool, efficient, minimal decoration.
All three share the same layout and component vocabulary; only type, colour and texture change. Each has light and dark modes.

## Anti-references
- Generic church websites: stock photos, gold crosses, heavy gradients.
- Flashy apps: neon colours, glossy effects, excess animation.
- The previous look: solid navy header bar with plain system UI, which felt dated.

## Design Principles
- The text is the interface: hymn words and scripture get the best typography on every screen.
- Readable before compact: generous size, contrast and spacing for older eyes.
- Quietly familiar: standard tabs, lists and controls; personality comes from type and colour, not invented widgets.
- Works anywhere: offline, phone or laptop, light room or dark.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in every theme and mode. Large default text with user-adjustable size, 44px minimum tap targets, visible focus states, full dark mode, respect prefers-reduced-motion. Twi characters (ɔ, ɛ) must render well in every font stack.
